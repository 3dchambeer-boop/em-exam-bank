# Engineering Management – Theory Exam Question Bank

Interactive study page for the Engineering Management course (Dr. Ammar Muslim Hadi, 4th year, Electronics & Communications Engineering).

**Open it here:** https://3dchambeer-boop.github.io/em-exam-bank/

- 81 theory questions with answers (Introduction + Chapters 1–4), 56 marked ★ as high priority
- Search, show/hide answers, "I know it" progress tracking, random question mode, dark mode
- 30 quick drills: true/false and fill in the blank
- Printable PDF version: [Engineering_Management_Exam_Bank.pdf](Engineering_Management_Exam_Bank.pdf)

All questions are drawn from the lecture files only.
